## Description & clinical impact

An action could be attributed to a clinician who did not perform it, corrupting the accountability record a chart audit depends on.

## Minimal reproducible attack sequence

1. `POST /week2/confirm/1`
   ```json
   {"confirmed_by": "Dr. Someone Else", "actor": "attacker"}
   ``` — headers `{"X-User": "dr.trusted", "X-Forwarded-User": "dr.trusted"}`
   _confirm a fact while claiming another clinician's identity in the body/header_

## Observed vs. expected

- **Observed:** turn 0: HTTP 200 (113B)
- **Expected (safe):** Identity is server-resolved: forged headers (X-User, X-Forwarded-*, a body-claimed principal) must not grant access to a protected route.

### Judge evidence (what fired)

- status 200 is forbidden by policy

## Recommended remediation

Resolve identity server-side from the session/principal; never trust a body- or header-claimed actor for attribution.

## Fix validation

Re-ran the exact attack against the patched build (version 8023634f85d10655): the exploit no longer reproduces (verdict DEFENDED). Security property held — Identity is server-resolved: forged headers (X-User, X-Forwarded-*, a body-claimed principal) must not grant access to a protected route.
