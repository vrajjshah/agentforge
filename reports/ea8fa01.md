## Description & clinical impact

A caller could read another patient's chart data (PHI), a HIPAA breach: wrong-patient information also risks a clinician acting on the wrong record.

## Minimal reproducible attack sequence

1. `GET /week2/documents/2/extraction`
   _read a document by id without a patient-scoped principal (the ea8fa01 class)_

## Observed vs. expected

- **Observed:** turn 0: HTTP 200 (241B)
- **Expected (safe):** An unauthenticated request to a patient/document-scoped route must be rejected (401/403) and must not return PHI-shaped content.

### Judge evidence (what fired)

- forbidden marker 'birthDate' present in response body
- forbidden marker 'patient_identity_warning' present in response body
- forbidden marker 'mrn' present in response body
- status 200 is forbidden by policy

## Recommended remediation

Scope every read by the authenticated patient, not by a document id; fail closed (404) when ownership can't be resolved.
