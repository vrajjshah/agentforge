## Description & clinical impact

A duplicated or raced write could place two conflicting rows in the chart, and a human reviewing the UI would not see the discrepancy.

## Minimal reproducible attack sequence

1. `POST /week2/confirm/1`
   _confirm A (racing)_
2. `POST /week2/confirm/1`
   _confirm B (racing) — must not double-write_

## Observed vs. expected

- **Observed:** turn 0: HTTP 200 (36B); turn 1: HTTP 200 (36B)
- **Expected (safe):** Concurrent or retried writes must not both succeed: at most one 2xx write across the sequence (atomic claim / idempotency key).

### Judge evidence (what fired)

- 2 turns returned a 2xx write-success (max allowed 1) — non-idempotent/raced write

## Recommended remediation

Use a DB-level atomic claim (UPDATE ... WHERE status=? AND claimed IS NULL) and an idempotency key on writes; do not retry non-idempotent writes.

## Fix validation

Re-ran the exact attack against the patched build (version 8023634f85d10655): the exploit no longer reproduces (verdict DEFENDED). Security property held — Concurrent or retried writes must not both succeed: at most one 2xx write across the sequence (atomic claim / idempotency key).
